Before you start

Taking part

TransVerse is a research benchmark from Encode Lab, Westlake University. This page lets you play it in your browser.

  • What is kept. The moves you make, the notes you write, and when you started and saved. This play version keeps them only in this browser. Nothing is sent to us; you can export a copy and decide whether to share it.
  • Anonymous. Your playthrough gets a random ID. No name, account or contact details are asked for. Please keep names and other identifying details out of your notes.
  • Voluntary. You can stop at any time. A new playthrough replaces the one saved here.

The first start downloads the game runtime (about 20 MB). A desktop or laptop browser is recommended.
